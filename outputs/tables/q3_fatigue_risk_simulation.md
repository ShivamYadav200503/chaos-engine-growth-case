**Executive Takeaways from Fatigue Risk Simulation:**
1. **Creative Fatigue is a Primary Financial Risk**: Because Q4 media spend operates near the efficient frontier, a 2-week production delay wipes out 2,194 installs and drops Day-120 ROAS from 100.36% to 96.90%, directly violating the CFO's non-negotiable 100% payback threshold.
2. **Severe Penalty for Under-Production**: Producing only half the required creative assets inflates portfolio CPI by +10.64%, eroding $13,548 in cash revenue and driving D120 ROAS down to 90.71%.
3. **Operational Pre-Requisite**: Marketing production must treat creative briefing deadlines as hard financial milestones. Batch 1 orders must be placed in Week 1, and emergency reserve creatives must be banked prior to the Q4 campaign kickoff.
