**Executive Takeaways from Fatigue Risk Simulation:**
1. **Baseline Anchored to Media Plan**: Baseline (On-Time Replenishment) reflects the exact Question 2 media plan (plan.csv): 75,545 paid MMP installs ($1.86 effective paid CPI; 78,839 total installs with the +4.85% organic halo), generating $140,391.63 direct D120 revenue (100.00% direct D120 ROAS; 104.76% blended D120 ROAS).
2. **Creative Fatigue is a Primary Financial Risk**: Because Q4 media spend operates near the efficient frontier, a 2-week production delay wipes out 2,605 installs and drops Day-120 ROAS from 100.00% to 96.55%, directly violating the CFO's non-negotiable 100% payback threshold.
3. **Severe Penalty for Under-Production**: Producing only half the required creative assets inflates portfolio CPI by +10.64% (from $1.86 to $2.06), eroding $13,499 in cash revenue and driving D120 ROAS down to 90.38%.
4. **Operational Pre-Requisite**: Marketing production must treat creative briefing deadlines as hard financial milestones. Batch 1 orders must be placed in Week 1, and emergency reserve creatives must be banked prior to the Q4 campaign kickoff.
