**Key Takeaways:**
1. **CFO Breakeven Ceiling ($140,392)**: The exact maximum spend satisfying the CFO's D120 ROAS >= 1.00 mandate on direct spend is $140,392 (delivering 1.0000x D120 ROAS and 75,546 installs).
2. **Organic Halo Extension ($158,000)**: Crediting incremental organic installs (+4.85% K-factor) expands the CFO breakeven threshold to $158,000 (1.0000x blended D120 ROAS).
3. **1-Year Cash Breakeven ($182,325)**: Deploying 100% of baseline spend ($182,325) pays back 100.0% within 1 year (1.0000x 1Y ROAS) while delivering $161,600 at D120 (0.8863x).
4. **CEO Full Budget Allocation ($250,000)**: Deploying the entire $250,000 maximizes volume (107,957 installs, $191,657 D120 rev, $216,427 1Y rev), but incurs a -$58,343 D120 direct deficit (D120 ROAS = 0.7666; 0.8096 blended) due to holiday CPI inflation and network diminishing returns.
