Data Dictionary

For the Growth Case. Back to the brief.
  • Games A and B use the same files and the same columns; only the file-name prefix differs (GameA_ or GameB_).
  • As of: all data is as of Tuesday 30 September 2025 ("today" in the brief). Numbers show what was known on that date.
  • Money is in US dollars.
  • Dates are calendar days in UTC.
  • A blank country means the source didn't report one.

Words used in many files

ColumnWhat it means
date / install_dateThe day something happened, or the day the players installed.
platformThe phone's system: android or ios (iPhone). A few rows show others, such as android-tv.
countryTwo-letter country code, lowercase (for example us, gb, jp).
networkWhere the player came from:
  • Net-A to Net-F: paid ad networks (see the brief, Appendix B).
  • Organic: no ad got credit.
  • Store-Search: credited to a search inside the app store.
  • Unattributed: the measurement partner couldn't tell.
campaign_idThe ad campaign on that network. unknown means the network didn't report one.
creative_idThe ad (creative) as the network reports it. unknown means the network didn't report one.
"Day N" (in cohort columns)The 24 hours starting exactly N days after each player's install time.
  • Day 0 is the player's first 24 hours.
  • Day 1 runs from 24 to 48 hours after install.

A1 / B1 · Daily spend and installs

Files: GameA_ua_daily.csv, GameB_ua_daily.csv. One row per day × platform × country × network × campaign.

ColumnWhat it means
installs_mmpInstalls that our measurement partner (MMP) credited to this source that day. Each install is credited to one source at most.
installs_network_reportedInstalls the network itself says it caused that day, using its own rules.
costWhat the network says we spent that day.
clicks_network_reported, impressions_network_reportedAd clicks and ad views, as reported by the network.

A2 / B2 · Cohorts: what new players did afterwards

Files: GameA_cohorts.csv, GameB_cohorts.csv. One row per install day × platform × country × network × campaign. Each row is a cohort: the players who installed that day from that source.

ColumnWhat it means
installsNumber of players in the cohort.
retained_d1, _d3, _d7, _d14, _d30, _d60, _d90, _d120Players from the cohort who played at least once during day N.
ad_rev_d0 … ad_rev_d120Total ad money the cohort brought in from install up to the end of day N, as reported by our ad-mediation software (N = 0, 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 120).
ad_rev_d180, iap_rev_d180Total ad money and in-app purchase money in the cohort's first 180 days (days 0 to 179). Same sources as the columns above.
ad_rev_all_sources_d0, _d7, _d30Total ad money up to the end of day N from all ad-revenue sources connected to our measurement partner: the ad-mediation software plus direct connections to individual ad networks.
iap_rev_d0 … iap_rev_d120Total in-app purchase money up to the end of day N, at the full price the player paid (before app-store fees), converted to US dollars.
payers_d0, _d1, _d3, _d7, _d14, _d30Players from the cohort who made at least one purchase during day N.

A3 / B3 · The game's own tracking, daily

Files: GameA_telemetry_daily.csv, GameB_telemetry_daily.csv. This comes from the game's own event tracking, not from the measurement partner. One row per day × platform × country (country as recorded by the game).

ColumnWhat it means
dauDaily active users: players with at least one tracked session that day.
new_installs / new_reinstallsPlayers whose first tracked session was that day: first-time players / players who had installed before.
banner_imps, interstitial_imps, rewarded_impsAd views logged by the game: banners, full-screen ads, and videos watched for a reward.
banner_rev, interstitial_rev, rewarded_revAd money logged by the game, by ad type (US dollars).
iap_revIn-app purchase money logged by the game (US dollars).
payersPlayers who made a purchase that day.
session_minutesTotal minutes played.

A4 / B4 · The game's own tracking: returning players

Files: GameA_telemetry_retention.csv, GameB_telemetry_retention.csv. One row per install day × platform × install type.

ColumnWhat it means
install_typeinstall (first time) or reinstall (installed before).
cohort_usersPlayers whose first tracked session was on that install day.
active_day1 … active_day30Of those players, how many had a session on the calendar date install day + N.

A5 / B5 · Ads (creatives), daily

Files: GameA_creatives_daily.csv, GameB_creatives_daily.csv. One row per day × platform × country × network × campaign × creative. Each network decides what counts as a "creative": a single file, a set, a pack, or just a type of placement.

ColumnWhat it means
installs_mmp, installs_network_reported, cost, impressions_network_reported, clicks_network_reportedSame meaning as in A1.
cohort_installsNumber of players in this row's install-day cohort (same meaning as installs in A2).
retained_d1, retained_d7Same meaning as in A2.
ad_rev_d0/d7/d30, iap_rev_d0/d7/d30Same meaning as in A2.

A6 / B6 · List of ads

Files: GameA_creatives_catalog.csv, GameB_creatives_catalog.csv.

ColumnWhat it means
creative_typeThe kind of ad (video, playable, image, a mix). It is worked out from our internal naming, not reported by the network. Where the name wasn't clear, the label says "possibly", "contents unknown" or "unclear". Treat it as a clue, not a fact.
first_seen, last_seenFirst and last day the ad had any activity in this data.

Templates

Template_predictions.csv (Question 1)

Each row asks for one ROAS figure for one cell and one group of install days:

  • ROAS means: the money the cell's players (installed in those days) brought in by day N, divided by the cell's spend on those same days.
  • Revenue is counted the same way as the CFO's rule: in-app purchases at full price, and the ad-revenue figure you judge correct.

For each row, fill in:

  • your_prediction, as a decimal (0.63 means 63%);
  • low_80 and high_80, a range you are 80% sure contains the actual value;
  • method_note, one line on how you got it.

Template_plan.csv (Question 2)

Fill in one row per network × platform × country for Game A, plus a total row:

  • the country code, or a name for a group of small countries (list the group's countries in note);
  • the cell's baseline weekly spend;
  • its quarter spend and its share of the quarter's budget;
  • your central estimates for the quarter's players: installs, money and ROAS by day 120, and money and ROAS after one year.
